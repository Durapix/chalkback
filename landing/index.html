The recap lands right before it matters

Students forget most of a lesson within days. Chalkback sends the key points, formulas and a small practice task, so the next lesson starts where the last one ended.

  • Written from the board itself, never invented
  • Formulas stay as LaTeX, rendered beautifully
  • Per-student language and channel
  • Idempotent scheduler: one reminder per upcoming lesson
